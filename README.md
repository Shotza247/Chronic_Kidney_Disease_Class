Welcome to my Chronic Kidney Disease Classification Model project. This project was built using anonymized blood sample data gathered from a private hospital in my hometown, Krugersdorp, across different age groups and medical aid membership bands. The dataset was fully anonymized before use, meaning all personally identifiable information such as patient IDs, names, surnames, ID numbers, addresses, and medical aid details were removed to protect patient privacy and confidentiality.

I was introduced to this dataset through a medical staff member who needed assistance with a master’s research project focused on chronic disease analysis. As someone close to the community and actively working within the Data Science and AI space, I saw this as an opportunity to apply machine learning to a real-world healthcare problem while also supporting academic research and responsible data-driven healthcare initiatives.
